# PoleNova AI - User Validation

## Validation Strategy
To ensure PoleNova AI solves a real problem, we must collect structured feedback from actual utility operators, engineers, and infrastructure managers.

## Feedback Template

**1. Background**
- What type of utility/infrastructure do you work with (e.g., rural cooperative, municipal utility)?
- What is your current role?

**2. Problem Validation**
- How do you currently detect outages in rural or remote segments?
- How long does fault identification typically take from the moment an outage occurs?

**3. Solution Feedback**
- Looking at the PoleNova Dashboard, which information is most critical for your daily operations?
- Would the "AI Explanation Panel" (showing confidence scores and evidence) increase your trust in an automated alert?

**4. Adoption & Roadmap**
- What is the biggest barrier that would prevent your utility from adopting this solution?
- What feature is missing that we should build next?

## Notes for Hackathon Judges
We are currently simulating sensor data to validate the software architecture and UX. Real-world validation with physical IoT devices is the next step in our roadmap.
